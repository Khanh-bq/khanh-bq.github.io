## Peer-Reviewed Publications

---

**[Lead & Corresponding Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc, Yen Bui Chu Bao (2026). Connecting the Dots: Causal Pathways and Counterfactual Learning Analytics in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. *Accepted at SCI 2026; to appear in Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, Springer.*
*Proposes an integrated pipeline unifying causal discovery (DirectLiNGAM) with counterfactual explanation to reveal interpretable learning pathways in intelligent tutoring systems.*

---

**[Lead Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc, Yen Bui Chu Bao (2025). Integrating Deep Learning and Explainable AI for Interpretable Educational Intervention Analysis. *International Conference on Multimedia Analysis and Pattern Recognition (MAPR), IEEE, pp. 369–374.*
*Demonstrates how combining deep learning architectures with SHAP-based explainability produces interpretable analysis of educational intervention effects.*

---

**[Lead Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc, Yen Bui Chu Bao (2025). A Time-Aware Framework for Explainable Causal Discovery in Personalized Learning Paths. *National Conference on Selected Problems of Information Technology and Communications (VNICT 2025).*
*Introduces a temporal causal discovery framework integrating preprocessing, multi-method inference, and SHAP interpretability to trace personalized learning pathway evolution.*

---

**[Lead Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc, Yen Bui Chu Bao (2025). Advancing Causal Discovery: Integrating Variable Grouping into ICA-LiNGAM with a Robust Preprocessing Pipeline. *International Journal of Advances in Science Engineering and Technology, 13(1), 129–134.*
*Extends ICA-LiNGAM with variable grouping and robust preprocessing to improve causal structure recovery in high-dimensional, noisy educational data.*

---

**[Sole Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc (2025). Proposing a CMM Model for Research Data Management at Vietnamese Higher Education Institutions. *Journal of Science and Transport Technology, 5(4), 1–8.* [https://doi.org/10.58845/jstt.utt.2025.vn.5.4.1-8](https://doi.org/10.58845/jstt.utt.2025.vn.5.4.1-8)
*Designs a capability maturity model aligned with FAIR principles for evaluating research data management practices at Vietnamese higher education institutions.*

---

**[Co-Author]**
Yen Bui Chu Bao, Khanh Bui Quoc (2025). Addressing Cold-Start Challenges in Personalized Skin Care Recommendation: A Hybrid Multi-Modal Learning Framework with SHAP-Based Explainability. *International Conference Green Finance for Sustainable Development 2025, pp. 939–950.*
*Presents a multi-modal neural architecture with SHAP explainability that mitigates cold-start limitations in personalized product recommendation.*

---

**[Sole Author]**
Khanh Bui Quoc (2024). Cosmetic Product Recommendation Using Enhanced Funk Singular Value Decomposition. *FAIR 2024.* [https://doi.org/10.15625/vap.2024.0197](https://doi.org/10.15625/vap.2024.0197)
*Proposes an enhanced matrix factorization approach that improves collaborative filtering accuracy for cosmetic product recommendation under sparse rating conditions.*

**[Co-Author]**
Trinh Bao Ngoc, Do Thi Phuong Thao, Dinh Thi Minh Nguyet, Bui Quoc Khanh, Nguyen Xuan Thang (2023). Student Selection to University Admission: Stable Matching Theory and Gale-Shapley Algorithm. *FAIR 2023.*
*Applies stable matching theory and the Gale-Shapley algorithm to model equitable student-to-university assignment in the Vietnamese admissions context.*
