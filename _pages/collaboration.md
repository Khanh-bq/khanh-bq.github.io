## Open to International Collaboration

I am actively seeking research and teaching collaborations with European universities, particularly through Erasmus+ mobility programmes and strategic partnership projects.

---

## What I Can Offer

**Teaching mobility (Erasmus+ KA171)**
I am available for short-term teaching visits (5–10 days) delivering lectures and seminars in the following areas:
- Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence (applied, with emphasis on interpretability)
- Data Mining and Learning Analytics
- Explainable AI and causal reasoning in educational contexts
- AI tools for research and academic work

All teaching is conducted in English.

**Research collaboration**
- Joint experimentation on educational datasets using causal discovery methods
- Co-supervision of student theses or research projects in AI and learning analytics
- Co-authorship on papers in explainable AI, causal inference, or recommender systems
- Knowledge exchange on research data management practices in higher education

**Institutional partnership (Erasmus+ KA220 / CBHE)**
Hanoi University is interested in developing strategic partnerships in AI-enhanced education and research data management. I can facilitate introductions to the relevant faculty and administrative contacts.

## My Institutional Context

**Hanoi University** is a public university in Vietnam with a strong English-medium instruction tradition. The Faculty of Information Technology offers undergraduate programmes in Computer Science. Students have solid technical foundations and strong interest in international academic pathways.

Vietnam is an eligible partner country for Erasmus+ Inter-Institutional Credit Mobility (KA171) and Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE) programmes.
