I am a lecturer at the Faculty of Information Technology, Hanoi University, where I teach Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Data Mining, Embedded Systems, and Python programming at undergraduate level — all in English. My research focuses on causal discovery and explainable artificial intelligence in educational technology, with particular interest in how interpretable causal models can uncover meaningful learning pathways in intelligent tutoring systems.

My current work integrates DirectLiNGAM and ICA-LiNGAM-based causal inference with SHAP-based explainability to produce actionable, interpretable insights from educational data. Complementary research interests include learning analytics, counterfactual explanations for personalized learning, recommender systems with cold-start mitigation, and research data management in higher education.

I completed graduate study in Italy at the University of Calabria and have participated in Erasmus+ academic cooperation activities. I am actively seeking research collaborations and teaching mobility partnerships, particularly with European universities working at the intersection of AI, education, and data science.

## Research Interests

- Causal discovery in educational data (DirectLiNGAM, ICA-LiNGAM)
- Explainable AI and SHAP-based interpretability in learning analytics
- Counterfactual learning analytics and personalized learning pathways
- Recommender systems with cold-start mitigation
- Research data management and FAIR principles in higher education
